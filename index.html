I'm Joshua Medina — a Lighting TD, Compositor, Technical Artist and Pipeline TD. I approach image-making through light, composition and the practical systems that support a shot.

My cinematography background informs how I work with motivated light and framing. My CG projects connect look development and compositing with procedural shading, render diagnostics and Python tools.

I completed my MSc in Computer Animation & Visual Effects at Bournemouth University, following specialised CG lighting study at Lost Boys.
