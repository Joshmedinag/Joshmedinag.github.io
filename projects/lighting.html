/ OVERVIEW

A landscape shaped by light.

For Broken Horizons, I helped turn the team's recreation of Tout Quarry into a cinematic sequence with a sense of solitude and melancholy. Death Stranding informed the visual direction: open landscapes, diffuse light, atmospheric depth and a deliberate, trailer-like rhythm.

My contribution combined lighting and cinematography. I developed around twenty working shots, with fourteen selected for the cinematic, refining light and camera together so the environment and its sculptures became the visual focus.

My contribution

  • Lighting research and visual development: overcast weather, fog, skylight, exposure and the relationship between scale and isolation.
  • Environment and shot lighting using Directional Light, Skylight, Sky Atmosphere, Exponential Height Fog, Volumetric Fog, Post Process Volume and Lumen.
  • Camera creation, composition and movement, with manual control of exposure, aperture, focal length and focus.
  • Shot-by-shot refinement of fog, silhouette, depth separation and focal points, including camera tests and alternative lighting setups.
  • A custom Movie Render Queue preset and final EXR render workflow, plus investigation of colour interpretation differences between Unreal Engine, Photoshop and DaVinci Resolve.
01 /

Establishing the mood

I compared interactive and cinematic workflows, studied rainy and overcast lighting, and explored shot flow and camera movement. Early tests helped establish a cold, solitary visual language.

02 /

Lighting through the camera

I moved from a general environment setup to individual shots. Wide views, sculptural details and foreground flowers offered different scales, while fog and exposure helped maintain a coherent atmosphere.

03 /

Checking the render

When EXRs appeared darker in Resolve than in the Unreal viewport, I compared them in Photoshop and investigated colour interpretation in post-production. This helped distinguish display and colour-workflow issues from lighting decisions.

/ BEHIND THE SHOTS

Inside the lighting workflow.

Render settings used for this project
Output
1920 × 1080 · 24 fps
Image sequence
16-bit EXR · PIZ compression · multilayer enabled
Sampling
32 spatial samples · 16 temporal samples
Quality overrides
Cinematic Quality Settings · texture streaming disabled · LOD0 forced · HLODs disabled · r.ScreenPercentage = 150

These are the settings recorded in my contribution report. The emphasis was cinematic image quality, with render tests and shot-specific adjustments supporting the final sequence.
